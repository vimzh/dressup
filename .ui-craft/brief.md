## Product purpose

Zdress lets shoppers try products on inside the retail pages they already browse, combine pieces across stores, and keep the looks that work.

## Primary user

A hackathon judge or online fashion shopper scanning the demo on a laptop and deciding within minutes whether the product is useful and technically credible.

## Principles

1. **The product is the proof.** Real extension states and retailer screenshots outrank decorative claims.
2. **Fashion, not fantasy.** Brand imagery should preserve garments, bodies, and materials instead of inventing generic AI spectacle.
3. **Explain once, then show.** Keep copy short and use visuals for flows that would otherwise need a paragraph.
4. **One accent earns attention.** The existing magenta accent marks actions and sequence; everything else stays restrained.

## Success metric

A judge can identify the in-grid try-on, cross-store outfit flow, saved collections, and system boundary without reading the full page.

## Out of scope

- Does not redesign the existing landing-page composition.
- Does not add decorative image galleries or stock-fashion photography.
- Does not replace authentic product screenshots with generated UI.
- Does not introduce a second accent palette.

## Learned constraints

- **2026-08-17** — Keep enough explanation for a hackathon submission, but replace nonessential text with useful visuals. *Why:* judges need technical context without reading a wall of copy.
